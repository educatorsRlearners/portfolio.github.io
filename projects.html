01

Identifying the Maturity Level of a Book to Improve Recommendations

When trying to predict the minimum reader age for a book, is the cover art or the book's details a better signal? I built and compared models using XGBoost, an LSTM, and computer vision on the cover art to answer the question — presented as a talk, "Using Machine Learning to Identify Text Maturity."

Web Scraping · XGBoost · LSTM · Computer Vision
