01

Write Like a Reader

Great writers anticipate their readers questions ahead of time and by answering them, produce stronger arguments. But how do you learn this skill?

Write Like a Reader is a peer-review activity which teaches students to anticipate what questions their readers would have before their reader sees it via a simple process:

  • a student gives their writing to a classmate
  • the classmate reads the first sentence
  • they think of 1-3 questions
If the next sentence:
  • DOESN'T answer the question(s), they write it/them down on their paper/draft
  • ANSWERS the question, they don't write anything down
The classmate then repeats the process for the rest of the draft (usually a paragraph or two) and when they finish the draft, they pass it back to their classmate who:
  • answers the questions
  • edits their draft to integrate the new details and reasons strengthening their argument
  • and/or revises their argument because they've changed their position after reflecting on it.
This web app simulates that peer-review process via a two-stage LLM pipeline where:
  • a "Questioner" agent raises reader questions after each sentence
  • and an "Answer-checker"agent verifies whether later sentences address them
This process surfaces unanswered questions as inline highlights so writers can revise toward clarity themselves. This app runs entirely locally with no accounts required.

Python · Gradio · Ollama (Qwen2.5) · SQLite · Docker
