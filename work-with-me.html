Work With Me Analytics Engineering & Data Science

Better Business Decisions Through Data

My deepest experience is in education technology, learning analytics, and digital learning products, where I’ve worked on personalization, experimentation, KPI frameworks, product efficacy, machine learning, and data infrastructure.

But the problems I solve aren’t unique to EdTech. Companies in any industry can struggle with the same questions:

  • Are we measuring the right things?
  • Can we trust the data?
  • What’s actually driving the outcome we’re seeing?
  • What should we build or investigate next?

I help organizations identify the right questions, determine whether they’re collecting the right data, and use analytics, data science, and machine learning to make better decisions. I’m available for the right full-time role, as well as consulting and shorter-term data projects.
